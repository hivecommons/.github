# Hive Commons Community

## Get involved

- **Dev & community list:** [hivecommons-dev@googlegroups.com](https://groups.google.com/g/hivecommons-dev) — discussion, meeting invites, announcements
- **Users list:** [hivecommons-users@googlegroups.com](https://groups.google.com/g/hivecommons-users) — operator questions and help
- **Security announcements:** [hivecommons-security-announce@googlegroups.com](https://groups.google.com/g/hivecommons-security-announce) — advisories only (report vulnerabilities to security@hivecommons.dev, never the list)
- **Maintainers (private):** hivecommons-dev-private@googlegroups.com — embargoed/security coordination
- **Community meeting:** every other Thursday, 10:00 AM ET — [get the invite](https://hivecommons.dev/meet) — [calendar (browser)](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=b43dc28a888d316aa1fe4a47bf3038cd1bca7bbb2b5cdbf5e7ecb9cc10672a95%40group.calendar.google.com) · [iCal](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/b43dc28a888d316aa1fe4a47bf3038cd1bca7bbb2b5cdbf5e7ecb9cc10672a95%40group.calendar.google.com/public/basic.ics)
- **Meeting notes:** [running notes document](https://hivecommons.dev/agenda)
- **YouTube:** [hivecommons.dev/tv](https://hivecommons.dev/tv) — meeting recordings and demos
- **Issues / roadmap:** [hivecommons/hive](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive) · [ROADMAP.md](../governance/ROADMAP.md)

Joining **hivecommons-dev** adds the community meeting to your calendar
automatically (the recurring invite is sent to the group).

## Community meeting

A 30-minute open call for contributors, spoke operators, and anyone curious:

1. Welcome + agenda scan (2 min)
2. Project updates — releases, fleet, migration status (10 min)
3. Community topics — issues/PRs/RFCs raised on the agenda (15 min)
4. Open floor (3 min)

Anyone may add agenda items to the notes doc before the call. Meetings are
recorded and posted to the YouTube channel; decisions land in the notes and,
where binding, as GitHub issues per GOVERNANCE.md.
