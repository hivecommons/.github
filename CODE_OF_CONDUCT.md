# Code of Conduct

The Hive Commons project follows the
[CNCF Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md).

By participating in this project — in its repositories, issue trackers,
discussions, chat channels, or events — you agree to abide by its terms.

## Reporting

- **Project contact:** conduct@hivecommons.dev).
- If your report concerns a project Maintainer, or you are not comfortable
  reporting to the project, contact the CNCF Code of Conduct Committee
  directly at conduct@cncf.io, per the
  [CNCF CoC incident resolution procedures](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct/coc-incident-resolution-procedures.md).

Reports are handled confidentially by the Maintainers, excluding anyone
who is a subject of the report. Enforcement decisions concerning a
Maintainer require a 2/3 supermajority of the other Maintainers, per
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Scope note on AI agents

Output produced in project spaces by an AI agent (including the project's
own hive-bot) is the responsibility of the human who operates it, per
GOVERNANCE.md. Conduct concerns about agent output should be reported the
same way as any other conduct concern and will be addressed with the
responsible human.
